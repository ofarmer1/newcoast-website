Why self storage

Why self storage continues to outperform.

01 / 04

Fragmented marketplace

Over 65% of storage facilities are owned by independent owner-operators outside of the top 100 operators. These facilities often lack the technological investment and operational sophistication necessary to compete in a rapidly consolidating market.

02 / 04

Resilience through cycles

Self-storage has generated the highest cumulative private market returns of any real estate asset class over the past 20 years. Need-based demand and increasing utilization rate have allowed storage to outperform through up and down markets.

03 / 04

Sticky tenant base

The average self-storage tenant stays for nearly 19 months and growing as lifestyle changes and cost of living have made self-storage use a low-cost extension of home. Tenants’ inertia once in place results in reoccurring revenue that quietly compounds over time.

04 / 04

Low costs, high margins

No build-out costs, low maintenance, limited staffing needs, and minimal capital requirements result in one of the most attractive operating margin profiles across all of commercial real estate. More of each dollar earned reaches the bottom line in storage than almost any asset class.
